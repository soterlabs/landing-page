The operating model

One accountable cycle from proposal to proof.

Critical operations should never depend on memory, improvisation, or a single person. Every Soter mandate runs through the same documented loop.

  1. 01

    Prepare

    Turn requirements into a clear plan, owner, and deadline.

  2. 02

    Verify

    Check calculations, controls, dependencies, and Atlas alignment.

  3. 03

    Execute

    Coordinate approvals, deployments, transactions, and delivery.

  4. 04

    Monitor

    Watch live systems, capital positions, milestones, and exceptions.

  5. 05

    Report

    Publish the state of the work and an auditable record of the outcome.
