Who we are

Built to protect what matters

Soter Labs is a governance operations firm for the Sky Ecosystem. We run the processes that keep autonomous on-chain organizations safe, compliant, and moving.

In ancient Greek mythology, Soter was the personified spirit of safety, preservation, and deliverance from harm. We carry that name because protecting what matters is at the heart of who we are.

We started Soter Labs because governance operations in on-chain ecosystems were too often opaque, ad hoc, and dependent on a handful of individuals. We believed they could be run like critical infrastructure instead: documented, repeatable, and accountable.

Today we operate governance end to end for Genesis Executor Agents across the Sky Ecosystem — from spell cycles and capital monitoring to Atlas compliance and transparent reporting.
